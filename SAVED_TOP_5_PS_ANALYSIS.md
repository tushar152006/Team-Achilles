## DETAILED BREAKDOWN PER CANDIDATE

### 1. PS 26083: Extreme Heatwave Early Warning & Human Thermal Stress Index
- **Sponsoring Agency:** Ministry of Earth Sciences (MoES) / NCMRWF
- **Real-World Crisis:** Over 3,400 excess deaths per extreme heat day in India. Traditional weather reports give dry-bulb temperatures, failing to capture deadly humidity + solar radiation interactions.
- **The Solution:**
  1. **UTCI / WBGT Physical Calculation:** Bio students model thermoregulation (metabolic heat generation, sweat evaporation efficiency, clothing insulation index).
  2. **Vulnerability Indexing:** Integrates ward-level Census data (slum density, elderly population percentage, outdoor laborer concentrations).
  3. **Municipal Action Dispatch API:** Automated operational triggers sent via WhatsApp/SMS to municipal corporations for cooling center activation, water tanker routing, and construction labor halt schedules.
- **Why Pure CSE Teams Fail Here:** Pure CSE teams only fetch OpenWeatherMap API and build a basic color-coded UI. They do not know how to compute radiant temperature or human physiological heat flux models.

### 2. PS 26111: Silage & Cattle Feed Quality Testing (BIS IS 19562:2026)
- **Sponsoring Agency:** Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution (BIS)
- **Real-World Crisis:** 47% of dairy samples in states like Punjab failed FSSAI safety tests. The root contamination begins with poor feed fermentation and Aspergillus flavus producing aflatoxins.
- **The Solution:**
  1. **Smartphone-based Vision Grading:** Evaluates silage color, moisture sheen, chop length distribution, and visible spoilage/fungal patches.
  2. **Biochemical Parameter Validation:** Input fields for farm-gate tests (pH test strips, temperature) evaluated against BIS IS 19562:2026 compliance matrices.
  3. **Toxin & Rejection Risk Engine:** Projects downstream milk contamination risk (Aflatoxin M1 levels) and issues immediate actionable remediation (aeration, binding agents, batch rejection).
- **Competitive Advantage:** Almost no hackathon teams touch cattle feed or silage standards because they don't understand agricultural biochemistry. Instant finalist potential due to domain uniqueness.

### 3. PS 26128: Livestock Disease Early Detection & Management
- **Sponsoring Agency:** Government of Maharashtra (Maharashtra State Innovation Society)
- **Real-World Crisis:** Lumpy Skin Disease (LSD) and Foot & Mouth Disease (FMD) cause billions in livestock mortality and milk production losses. Smallholder farmers face catastrophic financial shocks.
- **The Solution:**
  1. **Multi-modal Symptom Triage:** Structured symptom checklists + computer vision for skin lesions/mouth ulcers with confidence bounds.
  2. **Dynamic Epidemiological Containment Ring:** When threshold cases appear, an automatic 5-km buffer zone is established on GIS maps, alerting surrounding dairy cooperatives and veterinarians.
  3. **Bharat Pashudhan Synchronization:** Cross-references the 12-digit animal Tag ID to pull prior vaccination records and flag non-vaccinated herds.
  4. **Offline Resilience:** Local caching with SMS fallback for zero-internet rural sheds.

### 4. PS 26073: AWS Anomaly Detection (SkyGuard AI)
- **Sponsoring Agency:** Ministry of Earth Sciences / IMD
- **The Problem:** 3,000+ Automated Weather Stations suffer from drift, frozen sensors, and transmission noise, contaminating national NWP forecasting models.
- **The Solution:**
  - Autoencoders + Isolation Forests checking multivariate physical laws (e.g., temperature spike without relative humidity decrease is flagged as sensor error, not a heatwave).
  - SHAP-based inspector dashboard explaining the exact anomaly cause and predicting sensor replacement timelines.

### 5. PS 26165: SIF Precursor Detection in Oil & Gas
- **Sponsoring Agency:** Oil India Limited
- **The Problem:** Near-miss safety reports sit in unread quarterly databases. Minor slip-and-falls mask high-potential fatality risks (unisolated pressure lines, confined space entries).
- **The Solution:**
  - Fine-tuned transformer model categorizing text reports according to IOGP Life-Saving Rules, scoring SIF potential, and highlighting high-risk drilling clusters.
